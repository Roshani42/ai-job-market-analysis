Findings

What the data shows

01

Python and SQL dominate required skills, well ahead of Scala, Java, and R.

02

Executive roles pay ~2× entry-level roles on average.

03

Onsite, hybrid, and remote work are nearly evenly split — no single mode dominates hiring.

04

Top-paying titles skew senior and specialized — Data Engineer, AI Specialist, Head of AI.

05

Hiring is spread across many companies rather than concentrated in a few large employers.

06

Postings span 4 experience tiers — entry, mid, senior, executive — each with distinct salary bands.
